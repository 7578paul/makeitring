Something we got wrong

Four flat months, and a refunded invoice

We took on a mould and damp specialist in a market with roughly 400 relevant searches a month. We had checked that number and taken the job anyway. Four months later the booked jobs were flat, and the fee was never going to pay for itself out of that little demand.

We refunded the last invoice and told them to stop paying anyone. Two things changed for good: we add up searchable demand before we quote, and if our fee needs more extra booked work than the market can produce, we say no.

We have turned down three companies since.
