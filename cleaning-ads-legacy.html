Four things owners tell us on the first call

And what we do about each one.

01

A regular customer and a one-off cost the same to win

“A biweekly house is worth thousands a year. A move-out clean is worth two hundred dollars once. Google charges me the same for both.”

What owners say

We split them apart, because people search for them with different words.

Weekly cleaner and office cleaning contract on one side. Move-out clean and one-off deep clean on the other. Regular work gets the bigger bids and the better hours. One-off gets a cap, so it fills gaps instead of setting the pace.

02

The commercial work gets buried

“I want offices, schools, industrial units. What I get is forty people wanting a flat done before a viewing.”

What owners say

Commercial gets its own campaign, its own page and its own hours.

A facilities manager searches at 10am on a Tuesday. A tenant searches at 9pm on a Sunday. So commercial runs in business hours, sends people to a walkthrough request instead of a price list, and blocks the household wording.

03

You run out of cleaners before you run out of work

“I could take on more houses tomorrow if I had anyone to send. Two people just left.”

What owners say

We spend to the rounds you can actually staff.

You tell us how many you can add this month and we stop there. Work you cannot cover costs you a cancelled contract and a bad review, which is worse than a quiet week. Short-handed, we move the money to jobs that book three weeks out, and your hiring ads run separately so they stop eating the budget.

04

Price shoppers, and evening calls nobody picks up

“Everyone asks how much an hour, then goes quiet. And nobody is in the office after five, which is exactly when people ring.”

What owners say

The ads filter, and every call gets recorded.

Ads and pages lead with what is included and your minimum visit, so bargain hunters drop out before they cost you a click. And because every ad call is recorded, missed evening calls become a number instead of a feeling. On the last cleaning account we looked at, 6 of 9 calls after 5pm never got a quote.
