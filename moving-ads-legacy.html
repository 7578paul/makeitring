02

Local and long-distance in one campaign

“A two-bed across town and a four-bed three states away are not the same job, but they come out of the same budget.”

What owners tell us

Separate campaigns, separate budgets, separate pages.

Long-distance clicks cost multiples of local ones and deserve it, but only when the money is ring-fenced. On Advance Moving's account, long-distance and storage were 22% of spend and 61% of revenue in the first ninety days.

03

Month-end and summer, then the quiet weeks

“The last four days of the month and all of July we cannot keep up. Mid-February we are washing trucks.”

What owners tell us

Budgets follow the calendar you already know.

Up eight to ten days before month-end while people are still choosing, down once every truck is committed. In the quiet weeks the money moves to work that books ahead: packing, storage, office moves, property managers.

04

Quotes given, jobs not booked

“We quote all day. Half go quiet and I have no idea whether it was the price or the estimator.”

What owners tell us

Every ad number tracked and recorded, so the gap between an inquiry and a price on paper is visible.

On one mover's account, evening calls went to a mobile that took details and never sent the quote: 14 of 22 evening inquiries never got a price. No dashboard shows that, and no bid change fixes it.
