## ⚠ Performance Max ships without images

5 PMax campaign(s) carry headlines and descriptions only. A CSV cannot carry images, and an asset group **will not serve** until marketing images (landscape, square, portrait) and a logo are added in the UI. This is the same class of gap as the missing call tracking number — treat it as a launch blocker, not polish.
